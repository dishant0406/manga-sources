Summary

Taepung Shin knows every nook and cranny of his favorite MMO video game Paradiso like it was the back of his hand! However, his gaming adventures suffer an abrupt end after his untimely death. But this is not his last hurrah since he's reincarnated as Jared, a delinquent lord of a frozen province… in Paradiso!
